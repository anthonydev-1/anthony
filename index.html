Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. I want to be clear about what happens to your information.

What this site collects

Nothing. This site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and I do not receive any information about you when you visit.

Fonts

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When you open the page, your browser requests those fonts from Google, which can see technical details such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy for how it handles that.

My projects

  • Tabpad keeps your notes, tabs and settings in your browser’s local storage on your own device. They are not sent to me or to any server. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Tally runs in your browser. I do not collect data from it.

Hosting and other websites

My projects are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits under its own privacy statement. Links on this site go to GitHub and Yapin’, which are run by other companies with their own privacy policies. I am not responsible for what they collect.
